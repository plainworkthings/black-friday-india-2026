How to read this

Every row cites the page it came from. Live means a retailer or brand page, or a dated news report, shows the offer now. Upcoming means a start date is reported but the sale has not opened. Unconfirmed means only trackers or past years point to it, or an official page exists but states no terms. Percent-off figures are against each store's stated MRP, which can overstate the real saving, so compare the rupee price.
